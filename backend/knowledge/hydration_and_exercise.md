# Hydration and Exercise Performance

**Category:** Hydration
**Source:** American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM)

## Pre-Exercise Hydration

- Drink 16–20 oz (500–600 mL) of water at least 4 hours before exercise
- Drink an additional 8–12 oz (250–350 mL) 10–15 minutes before starting
- Urine should be pale yellow before beginning activity

## During Exercise

- Drink 7–10 oz (200–300 mL) every 10–20 minutes during exercise
- For sessions longer than 60 minutes, include electrolytes (sodium 300–600 mg per hour)
- Do not wait until thirsty — by then, performance may already be declining
- In hot environments, fluid needs increase by 50–100%

## Post-Exercise Rehydration

- Weigh yourself before and after exercise. For every pound lost, drink 16–24 oz (500–700 mL)
- Include sodium in post-workout fluids or snacks to help retain fluid
- Consume a recovery meal within 2 hours combining carbohydrates, protein, and fluids
- Chocolate milk is an effective recovery drink due to its carb-to-protein ratio and fluid content

## Overhydration Warning

Drinking excessive amounts of water without electrolytes during prolonged exercise can cause hyponatremia (dangerously low blood sodium). Symptoms include nausea, headache, confusion, and in severe cases, seizures. Follow a structured hydration plan rather than drinking as much as possible.
