# Bodyweight Exercise Routines

**Category:** Activity
**Source:** American Council on Exercise, CDC

## Benefits of Bodyweight Training

No equipment, no gym membership, and can be done anywhere. Bodyweight exercises build functional strength, improve balance, and can be scaled to any fitness level.

## Beginner Full-Body Routine (15 minutes)

Perform each exercise for 30 seconds, rest 15 seconds, then move to the next. Complete 2–3 rounds.

**Squats:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Lower hips as if sitting in a chair. Keep chest up and knees behind toes. Stand back up.

**Push-Ups (Modified):** Start on knees if needed. Hands slightly wider than shoulders. Lower chest toward the floor, then push back up.

**Lunges:** Step one foot forward, lower until both knees form 90-degree angles. Push back to standing. Alternate legs.

**Plank:** Hold a straight line from head to heels on forearms and toes. Engage your core. Hold for 20–30 seconds (build up over time).

**Glute Bridges:** Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Lift hips toward ceiling, squeezing glutes. Lower slowly. Repeat.

**Mountain Climbers:** In push-up position, alternate driving knees toward chest quickly. Maintain a straight back.

**Superman:** Lie face down, arms extended forward. Simultaneously lift arms, chest, and legs off the floor. Hold 3 seconds. Lower. Repeat.

## Progression Tips

- Add repetitions or time before adding difficulty
- Progress to single-leg or single-arm variations
- Decrease rest time between exercises
- Add a resistance band for increased challenge
- Aim for 3 sessions per week with rest days between
