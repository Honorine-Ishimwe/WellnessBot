# Yoga for Wellness Beginners

**Category:** Activity
**Source:** NIH NCCIH, Yoga Journal

## Benefits of Yoga

Research supports yoga for improving flexibility, strength, balance, stress reduction, anxiety management, sleep quality, chronic pain relief, and cardiovascular health. Yoga combines physical postures, breathing techniques, and meditation.

## Beginner-Friendly Poses

**Mountain Pose (Tadasana):** Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, arms at sides. Ground through your feet, lengthen your spine, relax your shoulders. This teaches alignment and body awareness.

**Child's Pose (Balasana):** Kneel, sit back on heels, fold forward with arms extended. A resting pose that gently stretches the back and hips.

**Cat-Cow (Marjaryasana-Bitilasana):** On hands and knees, alternate between arching your back and dropping your belly. Coordinates breath with movement.

**Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana):** Hands and feet on the floor, hips lifted high, forming an inverted V shape. Stretches hamstrings, calves, and shoulders.

**Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II):** Wide stance, front knee bent over ankle, arms extended parallel to floor. Builds leg strength and focus.

**Tree Pose (Vrksasana):** Stand on one foot, place the other foot on inner calf or thigh (never on the knee). Improves balance and concentration.

**Corpse Pose (Savasana):** Lie flat on your back, arms at sides, palms up. Close your eyes and relax every muscle. Used for final relaxation.

## Getting Started

- Start with 15–20 minute sessions, 2–3 times per week
- Use a yoga mat on a non-slip surface
- Wear comfortable clothing that allows full range of motion
- Follow video guides designed for beginners
- Listen to your body — never force a pose that causes pain
- Focus on breath synchronization rather than achieving perfect form
