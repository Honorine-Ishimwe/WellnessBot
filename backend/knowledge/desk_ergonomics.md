# Desk Ergonomics and Movement

**Category:** Activity
**Source:** Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), Mayo Clinic

## Ergonomic Desk Setup

**Monitor Position:** Top of screen at or slightly below eye level. Screen 20–26 inches (arm's length) from your face. Tilt screen slightly back. If using a laptop, consider an external monitor or laptop stand.

**Chair Height:** Feet flat on the floor (or on a footrest). Thighs parallel to the floor. Knees at approximately 90 degrees.

**Keyboard and Mouse:** Elbows at 90-degree angle, close to your body. Wrists in a neutral position (not angled up or down). Keyboard at elbow height or slightly below.

**Lumbar Support:** Lower back should have support. Use the chair's built-in lumbar feature or a small cushion or rolled towel.

## Combating Sedentary Behavior

Sitting for extended periods is associated with increased risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, obesity, and musculoskeletal problems regardless of exercise habits.

**The 30-30 Rule:** Every 30 minutes, stand for at least 30 seconds. Set a timer.

**Desk Exercises:**
- Seated marching: Lift knees alternately for 30 seconds
- Desk push-ups: Push against desk edge, 10 repetitions
- Seated spinal twist: Rotate torso left and right, hold each side 15 seconds
- Calf raises: Stand and rise onto toes, 15 repetitions
- Wrist circles: Rotate wrists 10 times each direction

## Standing Desk Tips

- Alternate between sitting and standing (30–60 minutes each)
- Stand on an anti-fatigue mat
- Wear supportive shoes
- Keep the monitor at the same height recommendations as seated
