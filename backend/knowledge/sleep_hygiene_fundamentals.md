# Sleep Hygiene Fundamentals

**Category:** Sleep
**Source:** National Sleep Foundation, CDC

## Key Guidelines

**Consistent Schedule:** Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, including weekends. Irregular sleep schedules confuse your body's internal clock and make it harder to fall asleep.

**Optimize Your Bedroom:** Keep your room cool (65–68°F / 18–20°C), dark, and quiet. Use blackout curtains, earplugs, or a white noise machine if needed.

**The 20-Minute Rule:** If you cannot fall asleep within 20 minutes, get up and do a calming activity (reading, gentle stretching) until you feel drowsy, then return to bed.

**Limit Screen Time:** Avoid screens (phones, tablets, TVs) at least 30–60 minutes before bed. Blue light suppresses melatonin production, the hormone that signals sleepiness.

**Caffeine Cutoff:** Avoid caffeine at least 6 hours before bedtime. Caffeine has a half-life of 5–6 hours, meaning half the stimulant is still active long after consumption.

**Regular Exercise:** Moderate aerobic exercise (30 minutes most days) improves sleep quality. However, avoid vigorous workouts within 2–3 hours of bedtime.

## Why It Matters

Adults need 7–9 hours of sleep per night. Chronic sleep deprivation increases risk of heart disease, obesity, depression, and weakened immunity.
