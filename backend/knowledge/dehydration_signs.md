# Signs and Effects of Dehydration

**Category:** Hydration
**Source:** Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic

## Early Warning Signs

- Thirst (by the time you feel thirsty, you may already be mildly dehydrated)
- Dark yellow or amber-colored urine (pale straw color indicates proper hydration)
- Dry mouth, lips, or skin
- Fatigue and low energy
- Headache or dizziness
- Decreased frequency of urination

## Moderate Dehydration Symptoms

- Muscle cramps or weakness
- Rapid heartbeat
- Sunken eyes
- Irritability or difficulty concentrating
- Dry, cool skin that lacks elasticity

## Cognitive and Physical Impact

Even mild dehydration (1–2% body weight loss through fluid) can impair concentration, alertness, and short-term memory. Studies show that dehydration reduces exercise performance by 10–20%, increases perceived effort, and elevates core body temperature during physical activity.

## Urine Color Chart

- Pale straw / light yellow → Well hydrated
- Yellow → Normal, drink water soon
- Dark yellow → Mildly dehydrated, drink water now
- Amber / honey → Dehydrated, increase intake immediately
- Brown → Severely dehydrated, seek medical attention

## Populations at Higher Risk

- Older adults (thirst sensation diminishes with age)
- Young children and infants
- People with chronic conditions (diabetes, kidney disease)
- Athletes and outdoor workers
