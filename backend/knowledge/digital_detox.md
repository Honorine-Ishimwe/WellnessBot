# Digital Detox and Screen Time Management

**Category:** Mindfulness
**Source:** American Psychological Association, Common Sense Media

## The Impact of Excessive Screen Time

- Average adult spends 7+ hours daily on screens (outside of work)
- Excessive social media use is linked to increased anxiety, depression, and loneliness
- Constant notifications fragment attention, reducing deep focus capacity
- Blue light exposure disrupts sleep cycles
- Doom-scrolling (compulsive consumption of negative news) elevates cortisol levels

## Digital Detox Strategies

**Phone-Free Morning:** Avoid checking your phone for the first 30–60 minutes after waking. Use this time for stretching, journaling, or a quiet breakfast.

**Notification Audit:** Disable all non-essential notifications. Keep only calls, texts from close contacts, and calendar reminders. Batch-check email and social media at set times.

**App Time Limits:** Use built-in screen time tools (Screen Time on iOS, Digital Wellbeing on Android) to set daily limits on social media and entertainment apps.

**Tech-Free Zones:** Designate the bedroom and dining table as phone-free spaces. Charge your phone in another room overnight.

**The 20-20-20 Rule:** Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to reduce eye strain and mental fatigue.

## Mindful Technology Use

Technology is a tool, not a compulsion. Before picking up your phone, pause and ask: "What am I looking for? Do I actually need this right now?" This brief moment of intention prevents mindless scrolling.

## Signs You May Need a Digital Detox

- Reaching for your phone first thing in the morning and last thing at night
- Feeling anxious when separated from your device
- Difficulty maintaining in-person conversations without checking notifications
- Comparing yourself negatively to others on social media
- Feeling drained after extended social media use rather than energized
