# Napping: Benefits and Best Practices

**Category:** Sleep
**Source:** Mayo Clinic, National Sleep Foundation

## Benefits of Strategic Napping

- **Improved alertness:** A 20-minute nap can restore alertness for 2–3 hours.
- **Enhanced performance:** NASA studies found that a 26-minute nap improved pilot performance by 34% and alertness by 54%.
- **Mood boost:** Short naps reduce irritability and improve emotional regulation.
- **Memory consolidation:** Napping after learning new information strengthens memory retention.

## Optimal Nap Guidelines

**Duration:**
- Power nap: 10–20 minutes (light sleep, quick refresh, no grogginess)
- Full cycle nap: 90 minutes (includes deep sleep and REM, wakes feeling restored)
- Avoid 30–60 minute naps (waking during deep sleep causes sleep inertia/grogginess)

**Timing:** The ideal nap window is between 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM, when the body experiences a natural post-lunch dip in alertness. Napping after 3:00 PM can interfere with nighttime sleep.

**Environment:** Use the same principles as nighttime sleep — dark, cool, quiet. An eye mask and earplugs help in office or travel settings.

## When to Avoid Napping

- If you have insomnia or difficulty falling asleep at night
- If naps consistently make you feel more tired (may indicate an underlying sleep disorder)
- Within 6 hours of your regular bedtime
