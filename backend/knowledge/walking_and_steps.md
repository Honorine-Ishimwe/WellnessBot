# Daily Walking and Step Goals

**Category:** Activity
**Source:** Journal of the American Medical Association, CDC

## Benefits of Walking

Walking is the most accessible form of exercise. It requires no equipment, no gym membership, and can be done anywhere. Research shows that regular walking reduces the risk of cardiovascular disease by 31% and reduces the risk of dying prematurely by 32%.

## Step Count Guidelines

**General Target:** 7,000–10,000 steps per day provides significant health benefits. However, even 4,000 steps per day reduces the risk of dying from any cause.

**Dose-Response Relationship:**
- 4,000 steps/day: Measurable reduction in all-cause mortality
- 7,000 steps/day: Significant cardiovascular benefits
- 8,000–10,000 steps/day: Optimal range for most adults
- Beyond 10,000: Additional benefits plateau for most people

## Strategies to Walk More

- Take a 10-minute walk after each meal (improves digestion and blood sugar)
- Park farther from entrances
- Take stairs instead of elevators for fewer than 5 floors
- Walking meetings (in person or on the phone)
- Set hourly reminders to stand and take a short walk
- Walk to nearby errands instead of driving
- Get a walking buddy (accountability increases consistency)
- Listen to podcasts or audiobooks while walking to make it enjoyable

## Micro-Walks

Research from the University of Columbia found that 5-minute walks every 30 minutes of sitting significantly reduced blood sugar spikes and blood pressure compared to prolonged sitting. These "exercise snacks" are highly effective and easy to implement.
