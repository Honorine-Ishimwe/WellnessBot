# Healthy Snacking Strategies

**Category:** Nutrition
**Source:** Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health

## Why Smart Snacking Matters

Strategic snacking prevents energy dips between meals, maintains stable blood sugar, and reduces the likelihood of overeating at mealtime. The key is choosing nutrient-dense snacks over empty calories.

## Ideal Snack Composition

The best snacks combine two or more macronutrients:
- **Protein + Fiber:** Keeps you full longer (e.g., apple slices with almond butter)
- **Complex Carbs + Healthy Fat:** Provides sustained energy (e.g., whole grain crackers with hummus)
- **Protein + Healthy Fat:** Maximizes satiety (e.g., a handful of nuts with cheese)

## Nutrient-Dense Snack Ideas

- Greek yogurt with berries and a drizzle of honey
- Hummus with carrot and celery sticks
- A small handful of mixed nuts (almonds, walnuts, cashews)
- Apple slices with peanut or almond butter
- Hard-boiled eggs with a pinch of salt and pepper
- Trail mix (nuts, seeds, dried fruit — watch for added sugar)
- Edamame with sea salt
- Whole grain toast with avocado
- Cottage cheese with pineapple chunks
- Dark chocolate (70%+ cacao) — 1–2 squares

## Snacking Pitfalls to Avoid

- Eating directly from large packages (use portion-controlled bowls)
- Snacking out of boredom rather than hunger
- Choosing snacks high in added sugar, sodium, or refined carbs
- Grazing continuously throughout the day without awareness
- Skipping snacks entirely, leading to extreme hunger and poor food choices at meals
