# Physical Activity Guidelines for Adults

**Category:** Activity
**Source:** WHO, CDC Physical Activity Guidelines

## Weekly Recommendations

The World Health Organization and CDC recommend for adults (18–64 years):

**Aerobic Activity:**
- At least 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week (brisk walking, cycling, swimming), OR
- 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity per week (running, HIIT, competitive sports), OR
- An equivalent combination of both

**Muscle-Strengthening:**
- Activities involving all major muscle groups on 2 or more days per week
- Examples: bodyweight exercises, resistance bands, weight lifting, yoga, heavy gardening

## What Counts as Moderate vs. Vigorous?

**Moderate Intensity (can talk but not sing):**
- Brisk walking (3+ mph)
- Water aerobics
- Cycling on flat terrain
- Doubles tennis
- Pushing a lawn mower
- Dancing

**Vigorous Intensity (can only say a few words before pausing):**
- Running or jogging
- Swimming laps
- Singles tennis
- Cycling uphill or fast
- Jump rope
- HIIT workouts

## Benefits of Regular Physical Activity

- Reduces risk of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and certain cancers
- Strengthens bones and muscles
- Improves mental health (reduces anxiety and depression symptoms)
- Helps maintain healthy weight
- Improves sleep quality
- Boosts cognitive function and memory
- Increases energy levels and reduces fatigue

## Starting From Zero

If you are currently inactive, any amount of activity is better than none. Start with 10-minute walks and gradually increase duration and intensity. The goal is progress, not perfection.
