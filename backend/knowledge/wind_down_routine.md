# Wind-Down Routine for Better Sleep

**Category:** Sleep
**Source:** American Academy of Sleep Medicine

## Building a Pre-Sleep Ritual

**Start 60 Minutes Before Bed:** Dim the lights in your home to signal your brain that nighttime is approaching. Bright overhead lights delay melatonin release.

**Warm Bath or Shower:** Taking a warm bath 1–2 hours before bed raises your body temperature. The subsequent cooling triggers natural drowsiness.

**Gentle Stretching:** Slow, gentle stretches (neck rolls, seated forward fold, legs-up-the-wall) release physical tension accumulated during the day.

**Journaling:** Spend 5 minutes writing down three things you are grateful for or listing tomorrow's tasks. This "brain dump" prevents racing thoughts in bed.

**Calming Beverages:** Caffeine-free herbal teas like chamomile, valerian root, or passionflower have mild relaxation properties.

**Progressive Muscle Relaxation:** Starting from your toes, tense each muscle group for 5 seconds, then release. Move upward through calves, thighs, abdomen, hands, arms, shoulders, and face.

## What to Avoid

- Heavy meals within 2 hours of bedtime
- Alcohol (disrupts REM sleep despite initial drowsiness)
- Stressful conversations or intense news consumption
- Checking work email or social media
