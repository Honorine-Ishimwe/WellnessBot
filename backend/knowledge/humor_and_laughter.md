# Humor and Laughter for Health

**Category:** Other
**Source:** Mayo Clinic, American Heart Association

## The Science of Laughter

Laughter triggers the release of endorphins (the body's natural feel-good chemicals), reduces cortisol and adrenaline (stress hormones), increases oxygen intake (from deeper breathing), stimulates circulation, and relaxes muscles for up to 45 minutes afterward.

## Health Benefits

- **Immune boost:** Laughter increases antibody-producing cells and enhances T-cell effectiveness
- **Pain relief:** Endorphin release provides natural pain management
- **Heart health:** Laughter improves blood vessel function and increases blood flow
- **Stress reduction:** A single episode of genuine laughter can reduce cortisol by 39% and adrenaline by 70%
- **Social bonding:** Shared laughter strengthens relationships and builds trust

## Ways to Incorporate More Laughter

- Watch comedy shows, stand-up specials, or funny videos
- Spend time with people who make you laugh
- Share funny stories or memories with friends
- Try laughter yoga (intentional group laughter that becomes genuine)
- Keep a humor journal — write down funny things that happen each day
- Do not take yourself too seriously — laugh at your own small mistakes
- Play with children or pets — their spontaneity is contagious

## Laughter Is Not a Replacement

While laughter has genuine health benefits, it is a complement to, not a substitute for, medical treatment or professional mental health support. Use humor as one tool in your wellness toolkit.
